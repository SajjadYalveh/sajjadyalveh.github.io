About

My research is situated at the intersection of private law, civil liability, and comparative legal scholarship. I am particularly interested in the legal conditions under which loss becomes legally compensable and in the situations in which damage remains uncompensated despite the existence of a legally relevant loss.

Research

Private Law

General principles and structural questions of private law and obligations.

Civil Liability

Liability regimes, compensability of loss, Haftung and the boundaries of damages.

Comparative Law

Comparative analysis of German, Iranian and European private law.

Liability Gaps and Uncompensated Loss in German Private Law

The project investigates the phenomenon of uncompensated loss and the circumstances in which German private law does not provide a corresponding claim for compensation. It examines the relationship between damage, compensability, liability and the concept of Haftungslücke.

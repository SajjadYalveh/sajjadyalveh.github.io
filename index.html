Current Research

Liability Gaps and Uncompensated Loss

My current research examines the legal phenomenon of loss without compensation and investigates when the absence of a compensation claim constitutes a genuine liability gap.

Liability Gaps and Uncompensated Loss in German Private Law

The project develops a conceptual framework for analysing uncompensated loss in German private law, with particular attention to the relationship between loss, damage, compensability, liability, liability gaps, and residual loss.

Academic Profile

About

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Law at the University of Passau, Germany, with a research focus on private law, civil liability, and comparative law.

My academic work examines the boundaries of civil liability and the circumstances in which legally relevant losses remain uncompensated. Particular attention is given to the structure and function of liability gaps in German private law.

Academic Background

I am currently conducting doctoral research in law at the University of Passau, Germany.

I hold a PhD in Private Law from Shiraz University, Iran.

I am also a First-Class Attorney at Law in Iran, with professional experience in legal practice.

Current Research

Liability Gaps and Uncompensated Loss in German Private Law

The research investigates the legal phenomenon of loss without compensation and examines when the absence of a compensation claim constitutes a genuine liability gap.

The project develops a conceptual framework connecting loss, damage, compensability, liability, liability gaps, and residual loss.

Comparative Perspective

My research combines German private law with comparative perspectives from European private law and Iranian law, with particular attention to the normative foundations of civil liability and the problem of uncompensated loss.
